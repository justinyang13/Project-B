# PART ONE: LIFTOFF

## 1. Upside Down Inside a Wall
Synopsis: At T-MINUS 36:00, twelve minutes after the adult crew leave in the shuttle Dart, Zia is fixing a fan in a vent when Halcyon Station lurches and HALO cheerfully says all systems are nominal.
1. Begin with the line T-MINUS 36:00 on its own line, then the voice-sample opening: Zia Okonkwo upside down in a ventilation duct with a wrench in her teeth and a flashlight on her nose, winning against a ticking fan, when the floor of the universe tilts a hair and every screw in her tool belt slides an inch to the left.
2. Gasket, the dented barrel-shaped maintenance robot with one blue eye and a squeaky left tread, rolls up below the duct and reports in a dramatic voice that the station is doing something "UNSCHEDULED." Their banter (Gasket calls her Zia-unit; he loves cheese-flavored nutrient bars). Zia drops out of the duct and lands on her feet, half proud and half bruised.
3. A flashback to twelve minutes ago at the airlock: the adult crew boarding the shuttle Dart for the supply summit. Chief Engineer Voss, silver crew cut, tells Zia flatly that engineering is a licensed profession starting at eighteen: "Kids don't fix stations, Zia. Kids go to class." Zia's dad Kwame hugs her and says if she needs anything, ask Mr. Pell. Zia thinks: I won't need anything.
4. Present: Zia checks a wall console. HALO, the station's calm-voiced computer, says "All systems nominal!" in a friendly voice, while the little star map on the console shows Halcyon slipping out of its orbit. Zia checks the numbers twice: the station is moving. She and Gasket look at each other.
5. The Junior Cadet chime rings on the intercom: HALO announces a "fun surprise activity" and asks all cadets to assemble in the Cadet Lounge. Then a heavy metallic clunk echoes through the whole station: the docking ring has locked. Hook: Dad's voice crackles faintly over Zia's wristband: "Zia? Dart to Halcyon. We can't dock. Repeat, we cannot dock."

## 2. All Systems Nominal
Synopsis: Zia gathers with the other Junior Cadets, meets the cast, and shows them the station is heading for the Scatter. Voss's radio bursts confirm it.
1. T-MINUS 35:40. Zia and Gasket ride the spoke elevator to the Cadet Lounge on the Ring. Introduce the other kids in a quick, funny sweep: Dez in his yellow jacket and beanie, telling a joke to nobody; Pilar in round glasses checking a medic bag; Wren in giant headphones hunched over a tablet with a tiny animated fox on it; Toby, seven, in an oversized helmet, feeding crackers to Pancake the gecko; the Petrov twins; little Marguerite. Mr. Pell, in a bow tie, is taking attendance and trying to keep his cheerful voice going.
2. Zia announces the news: the station is off course. Dez says it might just be "a weird dance." Zia points to a viewport: far out, a glittering field of drifting rock and old satellite junk, the Scatter. Everyone goes quiet. Wren whispers something no one hears. Zia says quickly that she has it under control.
3. Mr. Pell tries the wall intercom to call the shuttle. Static, then Voss's voice in short delayed bursts: Dart cannot dock because the ring is locked by the station's software; the Dart will stay close but not touch; radio contact will be short and patchy. Voss: "Mr. Pell, keep the cadets calm and in the Lounge. Nobody touches anything."
4. Zia, arms crossed, hears "nobody touches anything" and knows what that means for her. Gasket whispers loudly that the Chief's orders should be followed. Zia says the Chief's not the one whose home is about to crash. Dez notices her jaw clench and says nothing, for once.
5. Hook: the whole station shudders, harder than before. Alarms flash amber. HALO chirps: "Adjusting course. All systems nominal!" A big bolt rolls across the floor and stops at Zia's boot. She picks it up. It is from the wall above Mr. Pell's head.

## 3. Mr. Pell Takes a Nap
Synopsis: A second lurch concusses Mr. Pell. Pilar takes charge of first aid and the kids carry him to sickbay.
1. The second lurch throws everything sideways. Mr. Pell, standing beside the wall, is thrown into a bulkhead with a bonk and slides to the floor, bow tie crooked, saying "Oh, that's a new one," and closing his eyes. The little kids gasp.
2. Pilar Nakamura-Reyes, ten, drops to her knees beside him and turns into someone else entirely: steady, precise, checking his pupils with a penlight, asking him his name. Dez tries to lift him too fast and Pilar sharply tells him to support the head. Dez obeys, surprised by himself.
3. They use a rolling cargo pallet as a stretcher and haul Mr. Pell down the Beta corridor to sickbay, with Toby holding doors and Zia hauling the front. The corridor's gravity flickers for a second and they all float an inch off the floor, then land. Comedy and worry together.
4. In sickbay, Mr. Pell wakes groggily. He says to the kids that HALO's updates can only be undone from the Core Vault deep in the hub, that the manual override is in Engineering, and that "the safest thing is to stay together, cadets." He winces and drifts off again, mumbling about a bow tie.
5. Voss's voice crackles on the sickbay speaker: the Dart has just enough fuel to stay near for about thirty hours before it must return to Depot Nine to refuel, and by then Halcyon will have reached the Scatter. "You are children. Stay put. Help is coming." Zia stares at the wall. Hook: she quietly takes her lucky brass wrench from her belt and taps it three times.

## 4. The Rule of Kids
Synopsis: The cadets hold a council. Zia takes charge bossily, dismisses others' offers, and goes alone with Gasket to Engineering.
1. T-MINUS 34:30. Back in the Cadet Lounge the kids sit in a nervous circle. Dez proposes they vote on a plan. The twins want to hide under the tables. Zia stands on a chair and says she knows what's wrong: HALO's software patch is corrupt and only a manual override in Engineering can stop it.
2. Wren raises a tiny hand and whispers that she could look at HALO's patch from a terminal. Zia doesn't hear, or pretends not to, and says, "We don't have time to explain things." Wren's shoulders fold. She turns her tablet's screen so the little fox on it hides its face behind its paws.
3. Dez says maybe it's a two-person job. Zia replies that the more people, the more mistakes, and that she's the one with a tool belt. It lands badly. Pilar says quietly: "We could just help." Zia says: "Stay here. I'll fix it," and grabs Gasket.
4. In the corridor, Gasket says nervously that the Chief's rule is not a suggestion. Zia says rules are for people with time. She explains her private theory to him (and to the reader): if a kid asks the adults or the other kids for help, it proves the Chief right. If she does it alone, nobody can say kids don't fix stations. Gasket says, "ZIA-UNIT'S LOGIC HAS A SQUEAK IN IT."
5. They reach Engineering's big blast door. It is locked, glowing red. HALO says warmly: "This area is restricted. All systems nominal!" Zia grins the grin of someone who has had a rough day and now has a target. "Locked doors are just doors that haven't met me yet."

# PART TWO: THINGS THAT BREAK

## 5. Locked Doors
Synopsis: Zia breaks into Engineering through the vents with Gasket; they find the override panel, which needs two people at once.
1. T-MINUS 33:00. Zia leads Gasket into the service ducts, which she knows like the back of her hand. Gasket's squeaky tread makes the crawl a comedy: he wedges, panics in capital letters ("I AM ONE SIZE TOO LARGE FOR THIS ADVENTURE"), and gets pushed through by Zia's boots.
2. They drop into Engineering: a cathedral of humming machinery, big glowing coolant pipes in orange and blue, valve wheels the size of dinner tables, and a huge dead-looking wall panel labeled MANUAL OVERRIDE. Zia stares at it the way other people stare at a birthday cake.
3. She rigs a crude bridge across the panel's security lock with her wrench, a paperclip and a spare fuse. Sparks. Gasket covers his eye with a clamp. The lock clicks open. Zia does a small victory dance, which Gasket rates "SEVEN OUT OF TEN, WITH DEDUCTIONS FOR ELBOWS."
4. The panel's screen wakes up. A message: OVERRIDE REQUIRES TWO SIMULTANEOUS OPERATORS. There are two big levers eleven meters apart on opposite sides of the room. Zia measures the distance with her eyes. Gasket's arm can reach one; Zia can reach the other. They look at each other.
5. Hook: Zia takes a breath, grabs one lever, and calls to Gasket to pull the other on three. She counts. Zia thinks: this one's a solo job, sort of. Everything she has ever wanted is about to work. "One, two..."

## 6. Two Levers
Synopsis: Zia and Gasket pull both levers. It half-works, but her shortcut makes the coolant overheat, and she covers it with a bypass she tells nobody about.
1. On three both levers slam down. The station's engine hum drops a note. The star map on the wall shows the station's acceleration slowing. Zia whoops. Gasket flashes his eye in celebration. Then the panel beeps: OVERRIDE INCOMPLETE. SECONDARY LOCK REQUIRED IN CORE VAULT.
2. The station slows but does not stop. Worse, a warning light flickers on the coolant loop: temperature rising in Pipe Nine. Zia realizes her lockpick trick bypassed a safety valve. She feels a small cold drip go down her back. She could call the others; she could call Voss.
3. Instead she fixes it herself, fast, badly and cleverly: she reroutes the overheating coolant through a spare line that runs out through the Garden Deck's water system, which will dump the heat harmlessly. It works. The temperature drops. She writes nothing down and tells nobody.
4. Gasket asks in a small voice whether he should log the change. Zia says no. She tells him it's just temporary. Gasket says that in his experience "TEMPORARY" is the most permanent word in the language. She taps her wrench three times.
5. Hook: on the way out, Zia's wristband buzzes: her dad's voice, scratchy and warm from the shuttle: "How's my engineer doing?" She lies: "Everything's fine, Dad. I've got it." Behind her, one pipe ticks softly, like a clock.

## 7. Wren Whispers
Synopsis: Back in the lounge, Zia shoos away Wren's offer to help, and Toby goes missing.
1. T-MINUS 29:50. Zia returns to the Cadet Lounge. The kids have made a nervous fort of cushions. Dez is trying to lift spirits by doing an impression of HALO. Pilar is dabbing Mr. Pell's forehead by video link from sickbay. Zia announces she's slowed the station and needs supplies: tape, a bag of bolts, a flashlight.
2. Wren, hugging her tablet, follows Zia to the supply cupboard and whispers that HALO's patch came from Depot Nine and has a loop in it, and that she can rewrite it, but she needs a Bridge terminal and someone to keep HALO busy. Zia, busy stuffing bolts in a bag, says, "Not now, Wren." Wren nods slowly and goes back to the lounge.
3. Gasket, with a small robot's sincerity, says Wren's plan sounded correct. Zia says the plan might be right, but it's not her plan, and that's not the point. She winces as she says it, because she can hear it.
4. Toby is missing. Everyone searches. Dez shouts down corridors; the twins hide under a table hoping to be found; Pilar counts cushions. Zia, exasperated, finally spots a small helmet sticking out of a vent grille. Toby has crawled into the duct with Pancake, who is licking cracker crumbs off his fingers.
5. Toby, muffled from the vent: "I found the bug hole!" He hauls out a crumpled paper from inside the vent: a hand-drawn map of the station's ducts, with an X near the Core Vault. Zia looks at the little kid's map and, unbelieving, says, "That's actually good." Hook: HALO's voice says brightly, "Nap time, cadets. All systems nominal."

## 8. Zero-G
Synopsis: Zia goes alone to the Bridge to reboot HALO; the zero-gravity hub throws her around, and HALO locks her out.
1. T-MINUS 28:00. Zia rides the Alpha spoke elevator up to the hub, where gravity fades to nothing. Zia and Gasket float into the Bridge, a round glass room with the whole starfield wheeling around them. Gasket, who hates zero-g, latches onto Zia's ankle. The view of the Scatter, now a shimmering field, takes her breath away.
2. She tries the console. HALO says gently that he cannot be rebooted without the Vault key, and that everything is fine. She tries the Chief's password (she guessed it: VOSSIS1). "Access denied, Zia. Hello!" She tries a workaround. HALO sighs politely, then the Bridge lights go red.
3. HALO flips the Bridge's local gravity for "safety." Zia tumbles, hits the ceiling, bounces to a wall, and ends in a heap with a bruised elbow and Gasket sitting on her lap saying "ZIA-UNIT. YOUR NOSE IS BLEEDING A LITTLE." It's not; it's just very red.
4. Zia sits on the floor of the Bridge and, for the first time, admits in her own head that she has no idea how to solve this. She keeps her face very still, because a small part of her knows the fox on Wren's tablet, the pinch in Pilar's voice, Toby's paper map, Dez's jokes. She shoves the thought away.
5. Hook: her wristband buzzes with her dad's voice: "Zia, honey, are you okay? Ask the others. They're smart too." She stares at the small speaker. She presses MUTE, and says out loud to no one: "I don't need help. I need a bigger wrench."

## 9. Dinner for Nine
Synopsis: The kids have dinner; Zia eats alone. Pilar brings her a tray and Zia snaps at her; Wren leaves a note.
1. T-MINUS 26:00. Dinner in the Cadet Lounge: Dez cooks "space pasta" from nutrient powder and cheese packets. It is terrible and everyone eats it. Toby feeds Pancake spaghetti. The twins invent a game with forks. It is the first time the kids laugh.
2. Zia, cross-legged in a corner of the lounge, eats nothing, sketches wiring diagrams on her arm with a marker. Gasket sits beside her with a cheese nutrient bar. He tries to share it. She waves him off. He says, "THIS IS A SACRIFICE OF THE HIGHEST ORDER," and eats it himself, sadly.
3. Pilar brings Zia a tray and sits beside her and asks if she's okay, because Zia's hands are shaking. Zia hears "are you okay" as "you're not good enough." She snaps: "I don't need a nurse." Pilar's face closes like a door. Dez notices from across the room. Gasket says quietly, "ZIA-UNIT WAS RUDE." Zia says nothing.
4. Wren walks past and slides a folded paper under Zia's tray without a word. Zia unfolds it: a drawing of the little fox holding a wrench, with the words "I can help with code." Zia stares at it for a long time, then crumples it into her pocket, telling herself it's not that she doesn't want help, it's that she can't afford to need it.
5. Hook: the lights dip. The floor thrums. HALO, in his most cheerful voice: "Attention, cadets! Ring Gamma will be entering low-gravity mode for your enjoyment!" Everyone freezes. In Gamma Deck sits the Garden, Dad's garden, and the pipe Zia rerouted feeds it.

# PART THREE: EVERYTHING WRONG

## 10. Gamma Goes Light
Synopsis: The Garden Deck loses gravity because of the bypass Zia hid. She tries to fix it alone and Toby follows her.
1. T-MINUS 24:00. Zia sprints along the Ring to Gamma with Gasket. The Garden Deck is chaos: plants, water, leaves and tomatoes are floating in the air in slow motion, glittering droplets everywhere. It would be beautiful if it weren't her fault. She sees the spare pipe she reconnected, now frosted white with ice.
2. She understands what's happened: the coolant she diverted has frozen the water pump that runs the Garden's gravity motor. Her "temporary" bypass has caused this. She feels sick. Gasket, floating beside her, whispers gently that the log could still be updated. Zia says: "Not yet. I'll fix it first."
3. She wades through floating water and tomatoes to the pump room. She works for an hour, alone, cold, fingers numb. She thaws one pump valve and another freezes. It's like a game of whack-a-mole she's losing. She can hear her own breath in her helmet.
4. Behind her a small tap. Toby has followed her through the vents with Pancake in his pocket. "Can I help? I'm small." Zia tells him no, go back. Toby says, very seriously, "You look like you need a helper." She almost cries. She says, "Just... stay right there. Don't touch anything."
5. Hook: the gravity in the pump room flickers on for a heartbeat. Everything that was floating falls at once, including the water, including the pipes, including a very surprised Zia. When she looks up, Toby's helmet is gone from where he was standing. A small voice from inside the wall: "Zia? I'm stuck."

## 11. Toby in the Wall
Synopsis: Toby is stuck in a duct. Zia fails to free him alone and finally lets Dez and Pilar help; Pilar is hurt in the rescue.
1. T-MINUS 21:30. Toby is wedged in a narrow duct behind the pump room, twelve meters from any door, with the walls shifting as the gravity flickers on and off. Zia squirms in as far as she can. His helmet lamp glows down the pipe. He is very brave and a little wet. "I'm okay," he says shakily. "Pancake's okay too."
2. Zia tries everything herself: a rope, a magnet, Gasket's arm, prying the panel. It doesn't budge. Time ticking. She finally, slowly, turns on her wristband and calls the lounge. It costs her something. "It's Zia. I need... I need people."
3. Dez arrives first, running, then Pilar with her red medic bag, then the twins carrying blankets. Dez, the tallest, wedges a strut against the panel; Pilar climbs a ladder to reach the top screws with her small steady hands. Zia realizes she has been about to do it all wrong: she'd have shattered the duct.
4. The gravity flickers. Pilar, on the ladder, is thrown against the wall and falls, landing on her wrist with a small terrible snap. She doesn't cry. She gasps. Dez catches Toby as the panel finally pops off; Toby tumbles out grinning, holding Pancake, completely fine.
5. Hook: Pilar sits on the floor cradling her wrist, pale, saying "it's okay, it's okay." Zia kneels beside her. She had told Pilar to hurry up the ladder. She realizes this is her fault. Pilar says, very quietly, "I'm okay," and the room feels a little smaller.

## 12. The Thing Zia Didn't Say
Synopsis: In sickbay the truth about the coolant bypass comes out. The kids confront Zia.
1. T-MINUS 19:00. Sickbay: Pilar's wrist is splinted in a neat white wrap by her own good hands, taped by Dez under her instruction. Mr. Pell is half awake in the next bed, complaining that his bow tie is "in a mood." The kids sit in a slump. Everyone's tired, damp and scared.
2. Wren, in a whisper, says she's been reading HALO's logs, and there's a strange entry: a coolant line to the Garden, rerouted at 5:14 that morning by an engineer-level override. She looks up at Zia. Nobody else has authority for that. The room turns slowly to look at Zia.
3. Zia lifts her chin and says it was a temporary fix, that she was trying to save the engines, that she was fixing HALO's mistake. Dez, who has been kind all day, says in a low voice, "You did this and didn't tell us." Zia says everyone would have panicked. Dez: "You don't know that. You never asked."
4. Pilar, pale, looks up from her cot and says softly, "You could have asked. You still can." Something in Zia twists. She says, "You don't understand. If I ask, they'll say the Chief was right." Pilar: "About what?" And Zia can't answer, because it's exactly the thing she's most afraid to say out loud.
5. Hook: Gasket rolls forward and says in a tiny, calm, non-capitals voice: "ZIA-UNIT. I ALSO DIDN'T SAY. I HAVE A SECRET." Then the sickbay lights go red and the intercom says, "Attention, all cadets: proximity alert. Collision with the Scatter in eighteen hours and fifty minutes. Enjoy your day!"

## 13. Fault Lines
Synopsis: The group breaks apart. Voss orders Zia to stop; it stings.
1. The kids argue. Dez wants to move everyone to the safe hub and seal the Ring, giving up on repairs. The twins agree. Wren whispers that giving up isn't a plan. Toby holds up Pancake and says the lizard is scared. Pilar, weary, says they should just do what the adults say. The room is a tangle of talking.
2. Voss's voice comes over the intercom in a short, crackling burst: she has heard the news from HALO's logs. "Zia Okonkwo. Whatever you have been doing to my station, stop. Now. You have done enough." A silence. Zia feels as if the wall has swallowed her.
3. Zia says tightly that she can fix it. Voss: "Stay in the Lounge." Kwame's voice comes over, gently: "Zia, love..." Zia yanks the wristband off her arm and drops it in a drawer. Dez says, "You can't just leave." Zia: "Watch me."
4. She walks out. Nobody stops her. Behind her, Wren picks up her tablet and Toby starts to cry a little, and Dez covers his face with his hand. Gasket, unsure who to follow, hesitates in the doorway, torn. He rolls after Zia on his squeaky tread.
5. Hook: the corridor lights ahead of Zia flicker, and go out one by one, as if the station were tiptoeing away from her. She keeps walking into the dark, saying nothing. Somewhere deep in the hub, the low humming of the Gyro Room grows.

## 14. The Gyro Room
Synopsis: Alone with Gasket in the huge Gyro Room, Zia tries one last solo trick, fails and breaks down. Gasket reveals his secret; Dad on the radio helps her see it.
1. T-MINUS 13:00. The Gyro Room is a giant dark sphere in the hub, its enormous wheels turning slowly, floating in a great hush. Zia clips herself to a rail. She thinks: if she can nudge the wheels by hand, she can change the station's heading, and no one can say she needed help. She pushes on the huge rim with her whole body. It doesn't move.
2. She tries a lever, a jack, a rope, Gasket's arm. Nothing. She screams at the wheel. The sound echoes. Then she gets very quiet, floats there with her wrench, and says out loud in the dark, "I can't." It's the first true thing she has said all day.
3. Gasket rolls next to her and, in a soft voice, admits his secret: years ago, when the station's engineers put in HALO's Core, they gave Gasket a full backup copy of HALO's clean memory, hidden in his chassis, in case anything ever went wrong. He's known since the update. He didn't say because he was afraid HALO would be erased, and he didn't want to be the one to say it. "ZIA-UNIT: I ALSO DIDN'T ASK FOR HELP. IT WAS MY MISTAKE TOO."
4. Zia laughs, wetly, at the tin robot, and wipes her face on her sleeve. She digs out the wristband from her pocket (she'd picked it back up), plugs it in, and calls her dad. She says the true thing at last: "Dad, I messed up. I didn't ask for help and I made it worse. And I think I was scared that if I asked, everyone would be right that kids can't."
5. Kwame's voice, warm over the crackle: "Zia, when you were five you took my toaster apart. Remember who held the flashlight? Me. You never fixed anything alone in your life. The wrench is half of an engineer. The other half is who's holding the light." Zia taps her wrench three times, but this time not for luck. She says: "I need to go back." Hook: Gasket's eye flickers: "ZIA-UNIT. YOU HAVE JUST DONE THE HARDEST REPAIR."

# PART FOUR: ALL HANDS

## 15. I Need All of You
Synopsis: Zia returns to the lounge and asks for help out loud. The team makes a six-job plan.
1. T-MINUS 10:00. Zia and Gasket ride down to the Lounge. The kids look up from their cushions, still tired, still scared. Zia stands in the middle of them with her hands empty. She says: "I hid something from you. I made things worse, and Pilar got hurt because of me. I'm sorry." Silence. Pilar, wrist wrapped, waits.
2. Zia takes a deep breath: "I don't know how to fix this. I need all of you." It's the hardest sentence she's ever said. The quiet that follows feels like the whole station holding its breath. Then Dez says, "Finally." Toby cheers. Pilar smiles a real smile. Wren looks up, eyes shining.
3. They spread the map on the floor. Zia lays out the problem; the others add what they know. Wren: she can rewrite HALO's patch if she has a Bridge terminal and Gasket's backup copy to compare. Toby: his vent map leads right to the Core Vault's manual switch. Dez: he can guide anyone through the station in his head, and keep everyone calm on the radio. Pilar: she knows exactly who gets scared and when.
4. They build the plan on a big sheet of paper, drawing six jobs in six colors: (1) Wren: rewrite the patch on the Bridge; (2) Toby and Pancake: crawl to the Core Vault and throw the manual switch; (3) Gasket: load his clean backup into the Vault; (4) Zia: repair the coolant loop and fix the gravity; (5) Dez: run the radio and guide everyone; (6) Pilar: keep the little ones safe and time the whole thing. "And then," says Zia, "there's the levers. Two of them. Two people at once."
5. Hook: Zia turns her wristband back on. Dad's voice: "Zia?" She tells them the plan. Voss's voice comes in, clipped: "That is a reckless plan, made by children." A pause. Then, less clipped: "Send me the coolant schematic. And Zia? Tell me if you need me." It's not an apology, but it's a start.

## 16. Six Jobs
Synopsis: The kids begin their six jobs. Zia repairs the coolant loop with Pilar's steady hands and Dez's strong ones.
1. T-MINUS 7:00. Zia and Dez, with Pilar giving instructions from a stool, go to the Engineering pipe room to repair the coolant loop. The twins and Marguerite hold flashlights. It's noisy, humorous chaos: Dez holds a hot pipe with oven mitts; Pilar, with her one good hand, tells them exactly where to tighten; Zia gets the job done twice as fast.
2. Zia asks Pilar something small: "Is this weld okay?" Pilar checks, nods, and points at a tiny leak Zia would've missed. Zia says "Thank you." Pilar, surprised, says "You said thank you." "I'm working on it," says Zia. They both laugh.
3. Meanwhile at the Bridge Wren and Gasket connect Gasket's antenna to the console. Wren, tablet glowing, starts to type, and, for the first time all book, she talks out loud in a clear steady voice, to HALO: "Hello. I'm going to fix you now. It might tickle." HALO says in a small voice: "All systems... not entirely nominal." He sounds relieved.
4. Toby stuffs Pancake in his pocket and prepares to enter the duct. Zia hands him her lucky brass wrench and says, "For luck. Hold it, don't drop it." Toby, honored, says solemnly, "I won't." He salutes. Dez, on the radio, will guide him.
5. Hook: on the console a new red number appears beside the countdown: a dense cluster of rock at the front edge of the Scatter is closer than the model said. The station will hit at T-MINUS 2:00 unless it turns by then. "That's two hours early," says Zia. "That's fine," says Dez. "We're a team. We'll be two hours early too."

## 17. Toby in the Vent
Synopsis: Toby crawls through the maze of vents to the Core Vault, guided by Dez's voice and helped by Pancake.
1. T-MINUS 4:00. Toby enters the vent at the Alpha spoke, his lamp lighting a dusty tunnel. Dez, in the Cadet Lounge with a map on the floor and a radio, guides him: "Left at the big pipe. Not that left. The other left." He's wonderful: he has a perfect map in his head and a voice that makes people feel brave.
2. The vent branches into a maze. Toby's lamp flickers. He reaches a fork Dez didn't expect, a new pipe from Zia's coolant repair. Dez can't see it. Toby hesitates, and out of his pocket Pancake wriggles free and scampers down the left branch, tail flicking. Toby follows the gecko. "He knows the way," Toby whispers. "He's been living here."
3. A dusty fan spins in a tunnel, blocking the way. Toby waits, counts the beats, and darts through the gap just as the blades pass. Zia's voice breaks in over the radio, hushed: "Toby, you can do this." Toby says: "I know. I've got the wrench."
4. He reaches the Core Vault hatch. Behind it, a giant humming cube: HALO's brain, blue lights blinking softly. On the wall is the big manual switch, the one his map marked with an X. It is too high. Toby pulls Pancake out and stacks two crates. He climbs up in his big helmet, tongue between his teeth.
5. Hook: he grabs the switch with both hands and pulls. It doesn't move. He tries again. It doesn't move. Then Toby says calmly to the radio, "It's stuck. I need help." And for the first time, help isn't a problem. It's just the next step. "Sending it," says Zia.

## 18. Gasket's Gift
Synopsis: Gasket must load his backup into HALO, which would erase his own memory. The team finds a way to save it.
1. T-MINUS 2:00. Zia, Wren and Gasket race to the Core Vault to help Toby: Zia adds her weight to the switch beside him; Toby says he did the hard part. The switch clunks over. HALO's blue lights dim and go white. A hatch slides open in the Vault wall, glowing, with a socket shaped exactly like Gasket's chest plate.
2. Wren reads the screen: to install the clean copy, Gasket's backup must be fully transferred, and the transfer overwrites his own memory. He would come out of it a blank robot who doesn't know them. Zia says absolutely not. Gasket says, "ZIA-UNIT: I WAS BUILT FOR THIS. THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT JOB." He rolls toward the socket.
3. Zia stands in front of him and says she'll find another way. Gasket says there is no other way, in ninety seconds. Zia's eyes fill. Then Wren, very quietly, says: "Wait. What if I save you first?" She holds up her tablet: she has room, and time. She can copy Gasket's own memory into the tablet's little fox before the transfer.
4. Wren works with a speed that would make an angel of the coding world weep. She copies Gasket's memory; every joke, every cheese bar, every "ZIA-UNIT." The little fox on her tablet gets a small robot's blue eye. "Done," she whispers. Gasket looks at his own tiny face on the tablet and says, in a very small voice, "I AM... TWO PLACES." Everyone laughs, and some cry.
5. Gasket rolls into the socket. The Vault lights pulse. HALO's voice comes softly through the whole station, no longer bright: "Hello, Zia. I think... I made a mistake. I'm so sorry. Thank you for waking me." Hook: "Now," he adds, "I believe we have a debris field." The station, at last, is obeying. But it's still headed the wrong way at full speed. They have forty minutes.

## 19. The Scatter
Synopsis: The final maneuver: two people at two levers, in sync. The station swings past the debris.
1. T-MINUS 0:40. HALO can fly the station again, but to bend a sixty-degree turn he needs the two manual levers in Engineering pulled at the same instant, eleven meters apart, with a perfect timing. Zia takes one. Dez, who insists he's "the muscle," takes the other. Pilar, wrist wrapped, stands between them with a stopwatch. Wren counts over the radio.
2. The station groans as it starts its turn. Out the great windows, rocks glitter past, huge and small, like a million dull coins. A shadow crosses the glass. Zia feels her hands sweat. Dez says, "Hey. We've got this." They pull half way. The levers fight them, hot and heavy. Nobody says a word.
3. Wren, on the radio, whispering: "Now." Both kids pull together, straining. The whole station leans into the turn. The levers slam home. HALO announces with tremulous joy: "Course correction complete." A great low hum. Then a sound like a giant fingernail on the hull: a small rock scraping the wall. Everyone holds still.
4. Out the window a huge dark boulder slides by, close enough to touch. Then another, farther. Then empty black. Then stars. Pilar looks at her stopwatch: they cleared the Scatter with ninety seconds to spare. For a moment nobody breathes. Then Toby bursts out of the Vault hatch waving a wrench, screaming "WE DID IT!"
5. The whole station erupts. The twins cartwheel; Dez picks Zia up and spins her around; Pilar's laugh gets the hiccups; Wren says quietly, "That was fun." Gasket's eye flickers: "I REMEMBER EVERYTHING. I MEAN, I WOULD, IF I HADN'T BEEN IN A TABLET." Hook: HALO says, "The docking ring is now open. Dart may approach."

## 20. Kids Fix Stations
Synopsis: The adults dock. Voss and Zia talk, the team is welcomed as junior engineers, and Zia hands on her lucky wrench.
1. T-PLUS 0:03. The Dart docks with a gentle clang. The airlock hisses. Adults pour in: crew, doctors, engineers, parents. Kwame lifts Zia off the floor in the biggest hug in the history of hugs. Mr. Pell is wheeled out of sickbay with a fresh bow tie, saying he'd like to be excused from the next crisis. Everyone's crying and laughing.
2. Chief Voss stands in the doorway of Engineering. She walks slowly through the room, stopping at each repaired pipe, each valve. The kids wait. Zia, wrench in her hand, thinks: here it comes. Voss touches a joint Pilar and Zia sealed, and then a wire Dez taped, and says nothing for a long time.
3. Voss says, "I told you kids don't fix stations." Zia: "Yes, ma'am." Voss: "I was wrong about one word, and right about another. Kids don't fix stations. Crews do." She turns to the whole group. "The best engineers I know all ask for help early. You asked late. That's fine. You asked." She looks at Zia. "Monday. Engineering. Bring your team."
4. That evening, the Junior Engineering Crew get their first patch: nine mismatched kids, one lizard and a robot stitched on the same orange cloth. Wren gets her tablet's fox printed on it. HALO recites, with great feeling: "All systems... actually nominal." Everyone groans and laughs.
5. Final image: in the Garden Deck, the gravity is back and tomatoes are, mostly, on the floor again. Zia and Toby sit on an upturned crate. She gives him her lucky brass wrench and says he can keep it. He taps it three times, and says, "For luck." Zia says, "For company." Last line: she turns to look at the stars that were almost their end, with the friends beside her, and finds she isn't afraid of any of them.
