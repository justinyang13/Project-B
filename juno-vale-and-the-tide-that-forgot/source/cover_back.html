The tide has been gone for eleven months.
Only one girl is listening.

When eleven-year-old Juno Vale moves to Saltmarsh Harbor, the boats are stuck in grey mud, the harbor bell hangs silent, and everybody has stopped hoping the sea will ever come home.

Then Juno finds her Grandma Ivy's old shell whistle, and a very grumpy hermit crab in a chipped teapot starts talking to her.

With nine days until the Grand Low, a clever, nervous friend named Teo, a stranded sea turtle, and a sneaky developer who wants the harbor for himself, Juno must find three lost Tide Keys, wake a sleeping lighthouse, and prove she is a real Tide Keeper.
